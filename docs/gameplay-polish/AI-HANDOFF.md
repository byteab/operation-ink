# AI and actor implementation handoff

Files: `src/game/ai.ts`, `src/game/actors.ts`, `scripts/ai-checks.ts`, `scripts/polish-ai-checks.ts`.

The mission director now uses forward route movement with the lab walk/run clips. Routine and cautious search speed is 1.4 m/s; urgent investigation and combat repositioning use 2.8 m/s. Leg wounds multiply movement speed by 0.6, including animation cadence. The optional fifth `EnemyActor.update` argument supplies measured speed without breaking fake actors. Combat cover/flank/peek movement shares normal collision-checked routing. Unsupported strafing and passive sideways overlap pushes were removed. Congestion recovery turns and walks toward a collision-checked bypass, including backing out by turning to face that route.

Stationary orientation changes use the existing lab turn-step clips; actor-local clones remove the lab's fixed 60-degree hips cancellation because mission root steering is continuous. Movement waits until aligned with the route. Actors lower their weapon while moving/turning. Firing requires no movement, no turn, no active reaction, and 0.22 seconds planted before the normal weapon firing clock progresses. Open-ground guards close to about 18 m and hold for a useful firing window. Search expires and returns to patrol or the authored guard post.

Perception retains cone, height limit, distance, actual geometry LOS, reaction delay, bounded nearby communication, and last-known-position tracking. Tactical planning uses the last observed contact; hidden player movement does not alter destinations. Audible missed shots turn guards toward their source. Sound positions are projected from muzzle height onto the surface below for valid navigation. A visible muzzle disturbance may alert a sniper within its plausible visual range even beyond the ordinary pistol hearing radius; it starts investigation, not instant confirmed combat. Unsupported blind advancing fire was removed.

`role: 'sniper'` enemies remain at their elevated authored posts throughout combat/investigation/search. Their horizontal visual range is 110 m under the same 55-degree half-cone, <13 m eye-height difference, and real LOS rules. Acquisition builds at 0.55 suspicion/second, then a 1.4–1.7 second firing windup plus 0.22 second planted settle applies. Shared balance config gives 32 damage and 2.6–3.4 seconds between rounds. Accuracy is capped at 62%, penalized by target speed and wounds, and uses a gentler distance falloff than ordinary guns. Sniper post geometry/placement is owned by the integration package.

Enemy health and regional damage use shared `balance.ts` (`ENEMY_HEALTH=100`, `hitDamage`, `ENEMY_WEAPONS`). Every confirmed hit emits `enemy-hit` with its hit zone and replays the appropriate regional/left-side clip, including repeated same-region hits. Reactions advance just into impact on the event frame and freeze director root movement/shooting until the full-body clip finishes. Arm wounds reduce accuracy; leg wounds reduce speed. There is no damage immunity. Actor gun creation uses `createMissionGun`, enabling the shared sniper model without changing the character lab weapon registry.
